# CCNP ENCOR (350-401) — Core Enterprise

## Cisco Certified Network Professional (CCNP Enterprise)

> **Certification Level:** Professional  
> **Core Exam:** 350-401 ENCOR (Implementing and Operating Cisco Enterprise Network Core Technologies)  
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate → Advanced  
> **Recommended Experience:** 3–5 years working with enterprise networking technologies (recommended, not mandatory).

---

# 1. What is CCNP Enterprise?

**CCNP Enterprise** is Cisco's professional-level certification focused on designing, implementing, operating, securing, and troubleshooting modern enterprise networks.

Unlike CCNA, which teaches networking fundamentals, **CCNP focuses on real-world enterprise deployments** involving:

- Large campus networks
- Enterprise WANs
- Wireless infrastructure
- Advanced routing
- High availability
- Security
- Network automation

# 24. Common Mistakes

- Skipping CCNA review
- Memorizing commands instead of understanding protocols
- Ignoring automation topics
- Avoiding wireless technologies
- Not practicing troubleshooting
- Not building multi-router, multi-switch labs
- Ignoring design concepts

---

# 25. Best Practices

- Master the **why** behind every protocol.
- Build realistic enterprise lab topologies.
- Practice failures as often as successful configurations.
- Learn to read routing tables, adjacency tables, and protocol databases.
- Understand protocol interactions (for example, redistribution between OSPF and BGP).
- Practice interpreting **show** command output.
- Keep detailed notes in your Obsidian vault with diagrams and troubleshooting examples.

| Feature | CCNA | CCNP ENCOR |
|----------|-------|------------|
| Level | Associate | Professional |
