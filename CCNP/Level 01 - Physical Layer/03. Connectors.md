# Connectors

> **Level 01 · Physical Layer** — CCNP Enterprise (ENCOR 350-401)

---

## 1. Overview

Connectors attach the cable/transceiver to the device and are part of the **physical
media dependent (PMD)** layer. Choosing the wrong connector, or mixing multimode/single
mode pigtails, produces "no light / no link" failures that are fast to spot once you
recognize faces and polarity.

---

## 2. Core Concepts

### 2.1 Copper connectors

| Connector | Media | Typical |
|-----------|-------|---------|
| **RJ-45 (8P8C)** | UTP/STP (Cat 3–8) | Ethernet 10/100/1000/10GBASE-T |
| **RJ-48** | T1/E1 copper (identical face, keyed) | WAN circuits |
| **GG45 / TERA** | Cat 7 | Rarely seen; backward-compatible RJ45 |
- RJ-45 pins 1-8; gigabit uses all four pairs; pinout T568A/B matters only at choke points (both ends stable = fine).

### 2.2 Fiber optic connectors

| Connector | Type | Typical | Notes |
|-----------|------|---------|-------|
| **LC** | Duplex, small form factor (SFF) | Nearly all SFP/SFP+ module fiber runs | A/B polarity; SC keyboards in modular |
| **SC** | Duplex, push-pull | Patch panels / OSA | Older, larger; single-fiber-to-LC via adapters |
| **ST** | Bayonet, 2.5 mm ferrule | Legacy LAN | Twist-lock |
| **FC** | Threaded | Legacy telecom | Screw-on |
| **MPO / MTP** | Multi-fiber (8/12/24F) | 40/100/400GBASE-SR4, breakout, AOC | Polarity keys are critical |
| **E2000** | Shielded latching | Data centers | Denser standards |
- **SFP vs SFP+ size is identical** — LC twins attach the same; the module holds the laser/type (SR-single-fiber? no, duplex LC).

### 2.3 Optics / module connectors (transceivers)
- LC duplex on **SFP (1G)** and **SFP+ (10G)**.
- **QSFP+/QSFP28** use **MPO** for parallel (SR4) or LC-based CWDM4/LR4 (4-lambda duplex over pair of LCs) — it varies; **Quad** = 4 lanes.
- **CFP/OSFP/QSFP-DD** — 100/400G: 8× lanes, dense MPO/LC.

### 2.4 Handy mapping table

| Speed/Type | Module | Connector |
|------------|--------|-----------|
| 1G SX/LX | SFP | LC duplex |
| 10G SR/LR | SFP+ | LC duplex |
| 40G SR4 | QSFP+ | MPO-12 |
| 40G LR4 | QSFP+ | LC duplex (4 waves in 1 pair) |
| 100G SR4/LR4 | QSFP28 | MPO-12 / LC duplex |
| 25G SR | SFP28 | LC duplex |

### 2.5 Polarity / rules of thumb
- Two devices need **TX→RX**; through two patch panels they still must cross (A→B, B→A).
- **Never bend beyond limits** or use radius violations: macrobending of SMF creates loss.
- **Cleanliness**: spec on connectors; dust = high loss. "Check and clean connectors"
  is the first fix in optical fault isolation.

## 4. Exam Traps

- **SFP vs SFP+ have the same connector size/footprint** — differ in speed; a 1G SFP in
  a 10G port yields 1G.
- **MPO vs LC**: 40G SR4 = MPO (parallel), 40G LR4 = LC (WDM over one duplex) —
  don't mix.
- **"Connector" map questions**: put LC on SFP, MPO on QSFP.
- **Single-mode vs multimode connectors look identical** — the ferrule diameter differs
  (2.5 mm SM vs MM adapters). Wrong pigtail = huge loss.
- **RJ-45 vs RJ-48** same face, different keying (WAN).
