# STP (Shielded Twisted Pair)

> **Level 01 · 01. Cables** — CCNP Enterprise (ENCOR 350-401)

---

## 1. Overview

**Shielded Twisted Pair (STP / ScTP / F/UTP / S/FTP)** adds a conductive shield
(foil braid) around the pairs (or the whole bundle) to reject external EMI and
reduce radiated emissions. Useful in electrically noisy environments: factories,
data centers near power feeds, or with high-power equipment.

> ⚠️ Do **not** confuse with **Spanning Tree Protocol** (Level 08) — same acronym,
> completely different topic.

---

## 2. Core Concepts

### 2.1 Shield types (TIA naming)
- **U/UTP** — unshielded (plain UTP).
- **F/UTP** — overall foil shield, pairs unshielded (Cisco "STP" commonly this).
- **U/FTP** — each pair foil-shielded.
- **S/FTP** — braided overall shield + individually shielded pairs (best rejection).
- **S/UTP (ScTP)** — braided overall, pairs unshielded.

### 2.2 Why shield?
- Rejects **EMI/RFI** (there is no "lost" signal; the shield provides reference
  plane, keeps common-mode currents out).
- Reduces **crosstalk** between cable runs and against high-power parallel runs.
- Allowed **longer runs** where noise matters (still must respect Ethernet segment
  budget; shielding does not extend the 100 m rule for copper Ethernet).
- Required by many European EMC regulations and for higher-category (Cat 6a/7)
  dense deployments.

### 2.3 Grounding — the hidden exam point
- A shield that is not properly grounded becomes an **antenna**: it collects and
  injects noise, making things worse.
- Ground **one end** (typically the switch/patch panel side) to avoid ground loops;
  in structured cabling the grounding is handled by the patch panel bond.

### 2.4 Where do we actually choose it?
- Industrial/campus distribution where motors, VFDs, and high-power AC run parallel.
- Nearby fluorescent/arc lighting, heavy machinery.
- High-density racks where alien crosstalk (AXT) is a risk.
- International/EMC-strict sites.

## 4. Exam Traps

- **Shield doesn't exceed 100 m.** It fixes noise, not propagation attenuation
  (fiber is what extends distance).
- **Improper grounding is worse than no shield.**
- **STP = cable vs STP = protocol:** the exam may assume the protocol context on
  one question and the cable context on another; read for the word "cable."
- **Full-shield ≠ "better always"** — termination and grounding complexity and cost
  make UTP the default; shielding is a targeted fix for known noise.
