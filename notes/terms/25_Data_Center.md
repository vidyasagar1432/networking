# Data Center

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Spine** | Core switch in a Leaf-Spine topology — connects to all leaves |
| **Leaf** | Access switch in a Leaf-Spine topology — connects to servers and spines |
| **East-West Traffic** | Traffic between servers within the data center |
| **North-South Traffic** | Traffic entering or leaving the data center |
| **VXLAN** | Virtual Extensible LAN — MAC-in-UDP overlay for DC fabric |
| **EVPN** | Ethernet VPN — BGP-based control plane for VXLAN |
